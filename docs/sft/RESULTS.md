# Phase 3: supervised fine-tuning results

The pretrained 33.6M-parameter L model fine-tuned on TinyStories-Instruct: 300,000 examples (82.0M tokens), each an instruction prompt followed by its story and an end-of-text token, with the loss on the story and end-of-text tokens only. 1,400 steps of 65,536 tokens (1.12 passes over the data), peak learning rate 0.0003 with 50 warmup steps and cosine decay to 10%, bf16. Generated by `scripts/phase3_report.py` from `results/sft/`.

## Instruction following

The 500 fixed held-out prompts (drawn from the validation split, never trained on), 4 samples each at temperature 0.8 and top-p 0.95. A sample satisfies its prompt when every checkable constraint passes: each required word appears (inflections allowed), the given sentence appears verbatim, and there is quoted speech when Dialogue is requested. The gold column scores the dataset's own story for each prompt with the same checks.

| | Base L | SFT L | Gold stories |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **All constraints met** | **1.8%** | **35.4%** | **95.2%** |
| Mean reward (fraction of constraints met) | 0.144 | 0.686 | 0.982 |
| Required words found (per word) | 17.9% | 77.7% | 99.0% |
| All required words found | 1.0% | 47.3% | 97.0% |
| Given sentence included | 0.1% | 11.6% | 100.0% |
| Dialogue when requested | 20.9% | 95.5% | 94.7% |
| Story ended with end-of-text | 99.7% | 93.8% | 100.0% |
| Fluency: nats/token under pretrained L | 2.319 | 0.966 | 1.218 |
| Mean length (words) | 44 | 174 | 172 |
| Repeated word 4-grams | 0.6% | 2.0% | 1.1% |

Constraint counts: 401 prompts with required words, 186 prompts with a given sentence, 244 prompts with Dialogue. For scale, a gold story scored against a different prompt's constraints satisfies them 3.8% of the time (whole validation split, `results/instruct/gold_check.json`).

SFT raised the share of fully satisfied samples from 1.8% to 35.4% and the mean reward from 0.144 to 0.686. Dialogue is almost always there when asked for (95.5%), and most required words appear (77.7% of them), but all of a prompt's words only in 47.3% of samples, and the given sentence in just 11.6%: copying a sentence verbatim is the constraint SFT learned least.

Fluency is each story's loss under the pretrained L, scored on its own as in pretraining. It compares models sampled the same way, not models against the gold text: sampling at temperature 0.8 with top-p favors probable tokens, which is why SFT samples (0.966) score lower than the human-written gold stories (1.218). Base L samples (2.319) are short continuations of an instruction format it never saw, not stories.

## Training

Validation loss on the story tokens of 5,000 held-out examples fell from 1.3530 (the base model) to 1.0380 nats/token (0.3689 bits per byte of story text):

| Step | 100 | 200 | 400 | 600 | 800 | 1000 | 1200 | 1400 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Validation loss | 1.1161 | 1.1000 | 1.0794 | 1.0672 | 1.0567 | 1.0466 | 1.0407 | 1.0380 |

Training took 75 minutes on the RTX 4050, including 534 thermal-guard pauses (26 minutes) at 85 °C.

## What went wrong first

The first evaluation reported 5.1% satisfied for SFT L, with only 14.5% of stories finished. Nearly all of the unfinished ones were empty strings. The generation command queued all 2,000 requests before the first engine step, and the engine, written for serving, rejects requests beyond a 1,024-request queue and drops requests that wait more than 60 s. 1,695 SFT samples and 1,303 base samples came back empty and were scored as failures. Offline generation now uses an engine without those limits and raises if any request fails, with a regression test that makes every engine step take 100 s. Counts: `results/sft/first_eval_dropped_requests.json`.
